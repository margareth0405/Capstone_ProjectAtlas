ATLAS is an academic library platform. The responsible institution should configure its exact legal name and address before public deployment.

2. Privacy policy and data minimization

ATLAS collects only information used to operate and protect the academic library: name, email address, student or teacher role, securely hashed password, consent records, bookmarks, contact requests, protected-resource viewing records, and limited session or security information such as IP address, page path, and download user agent.

ATLAS does not ask for a birth date, government identification number, payment-card information, precise location, or advertising profile. Administrators should not upload those details into free-text fields. Personal information is used for authentication, requested library functions, support, security, accountability, and institutional library operations. It is not sold.

These practices follow the principles of transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality described in the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

3. Minors and parent or guardian confirmation

ATLAS may be used by students who are minors. Registration requires the user to confirm that they are legally able to consent or that a parent or legal guardian has authorized the account and reviewed this notice. ATLAS records the confirmation time and policy version, but does not collect a birth date solely for this check.

A parent or legal guardian may submit a privacy, access, correction, or deletion request for a minor under their care. ATLAS may request reasonable identity or authority verification before changing account data. The National Privacy Commission explains that parents or guardians protect the privacy rights of minors under their care in its data-subject rights guidance.

4. Cookie policy and consent banner

Essential cookies support sign-in, CSRF protection, security, saved cookie choices, and guest sessions. They are necessary for requested features and cannot be disabled through the banner.

Optional first-party usage analytics records page navigation and active-session duration for authorized library staff. It begins only after “Allow analytics” is selected. “Essential only” is presented as an equally available choice, and the decision can be changed at any time.

6. Retention and security

Records are retained only while needed for account operation, requested support, service security, accountable library administration, institutional requirements, or applicable obligations. Deletion requests are reviewed against those needs rather than being silently ignored or automatically denied.

ATLAS uses access controls, password hashing, secure production cookies, HTTPS enforcement, input and upload validation, rate limits, restricted media routes, and audit records. Submitted AI Detection text and uploaded analysis documents are processed in memory and are not retained in the analysis record.

7. Access, correction, objection, and data deletion requests

You may request information about your personal data, ask for a correction, object to processing, or ask ATLAS to delete or block eligible data. Some records may need to be retained for a legal obligation, a valid institutional requirement, security, or the establishment or defense of a claim. See the National Privacy Commission’s right to erasure or blocking guidance.

Submit a data deletion request

ATLAS may verify the requester’s identity before disclosing, correcting, blocking, or deleting account information. Requests and responses are recorded for accountability.

8. Terms of service

  • Use ATLAS only for lawful academic, teaching, learning, and library purposes.
  • Keep account credentials private and do not attempt to access another person’s account.
  • Respect copyright, licensing conditions, confidentiality, and restrictions shown with library resources.
  • Do not upload malware, send spam, automate abusive requests, scrape protected materials, or interfere with service availability.
  • Access may be limited or suspended when reasonably necessary to protect users, content, institutional operations, or the service.

9. Fees, reviews, and service claims

ATLAS currently has no checkout, purchase, paid subscription, advertising, or payment-card feature. It therefore presents no service charges or hidden fees.

ATLAS does not publish customer ratings, testimonials, or user reviews, so it does not generate or display fabricated reviews. Catalog descriptions and announcements must identify their source and must not claim endorsements, guaranteed outcomes, or capabilities that cannot be supported.

Service availability and automated results are not guaranteed to be error-free. Maintenance, connectivity, source-file quality, and model limitations can affect results.

10. Accessibility

ATLAS provides a skip link, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus indicators, descriptive page titles and labels, alternative text for meaningful cover images, hidden treatment for decorative icons, responsive layouts, and persistent Large text and High contrast controls in the Display menu.

If a page, document, or control is difficult to use, submit an accessibility request through Library Support.

11. Fonts, icons, images, and software licenses

  • Bootstrap code is used under the MIT License.
  • Font Awesome Free icons, fonts, and code are used under the licenses listed in the Font Awesome Free License.
  • The ATLAS favicon is a project interface asset. Catalog covers are supplied by authorized administrators; the uploader must have permission to use each image.
  • Uploaded library materials remain subject to their copyright and institutional permissions. ATLAS does not claim ownership merely because a resource is listed.

12. Email choices

ATLAS sends requested account and service messages such as email verification, password recovery, and support responses. It does not currently operate a promotional newsletter or automatically enroll users in marketing email.

Required security messages cannot be disabled while the related account action is pending. To question an email, change optional email preferences, or request account deletion, use the email-preferences request.

13. AI Detection

AI Detection produces an automated estimate that can be wrong. It is not proof of authorship, does not guarantee an academic conclusion, and must not be the sole basis for a penalty. Authorized reviewers must consider context and other evidence.

This page describes the implemented ATLAS configuration. The responsible institution must insert its exact legal operator name and address and obtain a qualified local review before public launch.
